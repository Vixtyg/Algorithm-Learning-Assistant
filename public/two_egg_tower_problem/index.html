The two egg tower problem

The core problem is the following:

You are in a house with n floors. You also have two eggs. You want to find the lowest floor, after which your eggs start to break.


You can reuse your eggs as many times as you want. However, after you drop them from a "deadly floor" or higher, they will break. After that, they will NOT be reusable!

If you break all the eggs, you have to restart


Your job is to...
  • Develop an Algorithm for finding the deadly floor!
  • Describe the time complexity of your algorithm! (how many tests for n-floors)
Drops:
After dropping one egg, you have to iterate through each remaining floor. That means that you have a time complexity of O(m) of m remaining elements
You obviously have to skip some floors. What happens if you skip too many floors? Or if you dont skip enough? What is the relationship between big and small jumps?
Halving the floors all the time is not really effective. In the worst case, you will have a time complexity of O(n/2). There is a more efficient way!
Binary search will not help you here. The length of big jumps in a house with n floors remains constant
Try to divide the time complexity into the time complexity of big jumps (skipping many floors) and small jumps (going up one floor). For example, skipping every 10 floors is O(b/10). How many smalls steps will remain?
